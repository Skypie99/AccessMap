Know before you go.

Flagstone is a community map of accessibility barriers — missing ramps, broken sidewalks, blocked paths — reported and verified by the people who face them.

Every report on Flagstone is a real barrier, described by a real person, and verified by the community. Browse the map without an account; sign in only when you want to report, verify, or comment. No ads, no analytics, no trackers.
