About AccessMap

AccessMap is a crowdsourced mobile app for mapping accessibility barriers in the real world. Users drop pins at locations where there's an accessibility issue — a missing ramp, a broken sidewalk, an inaccessible parking spot — and the community verifies, resolves, or rejects those reports. The goal is to make cities and neighborhoods easier to navigate for everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report an accessibility barrier?

Open the app and tap the + button (bottom-right of the map screen). Choose the barrier category (ramp, sidewalk, parking, etc.), set the severity, write a short description, and optionally add a photo. Tap Submit — your flag will appear on the map immediately. Other users can then verify or resolve it.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your email, display name, all flags you've submitted, and all associated photos. This action cannot be undone. If you'd prefer, you can also email us at [contact email] and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.

Why does the app need my location?

AccessMap uses your location only while the app is open — to center the map on where you are and to make it easier to place flags at the right spot. We never access your location in the background, and we never store or share your device's real-time location. When you submit a flag, we only store the specific latitude/longitude you chose for that flag.

Are my photos private?

Photos attached to flags are publicly visible on the map — anyone using AccessMap can see them. Before storing your photo, we automatically strip all EXIF metadata (including GPS coordinates, timestamps, and camera model) so none of that information is ever stored or visible to others.

What do the flag statuses mean?

Open — newly submitted, needs community verification. Verified — at least one other user has confirmed the barrier exists. Resolved — the barrier has been fixed or removed. Rejected — the flag was determined to be inaccurate or a duplicate.

How do I earn points?

You earn points by contributing to the community: +5 when a flag you submitted gets verified, +10 when it gets resolved. You also earn +2 for verifying someone else's flag and +5 for resolving one. Points reflect your community contribution and appear on your profile.

Does the app work on web / without the mobile app?

Yes — AccessMap has a web version that runs in any modern browser. You can view the map without an account. Reporting and verifying flags requires signing in, which works the same way on web as in the mobile app.

Is AccessMap available on Android?

The iOS app is the primary release. An Android version is planned — check back for updates or join the beta testing program by emailing us.
