# AccessMap v0.2.0 — Beta Release Notes

**Release date:** 2026-05-30  
**Type:** Beta (invite-only testing)

Thanks for testing AccessMap! Here's what we've added since v0.1.0.

---

## What's New

### 🎯 Smarter Map with Clustering
When you zoom out, flags that are close together now group into a single bubble showing the count. Tap a cluster to zoom in and see each individual flag. This makes it way easier to navigate busy areas without the map turning into a sea of pins.

### 📅 Flag Timeline
Tap a flag detail to see its full history — "Reported → Verified → Resolved" — so you can track what the community has done about it.

### 📡 Push Notifications (iOS)
Get a push alert when one of your reported flags is verified or resolved. You control this in **Settings → Notifications**. Turn it on or off anytime.

### 📍 Offline Map Cache
The map stores recently viewed tiles on your phone for 7 days, so the map stays fast and works even when your signal is spotty. The cache clears when you sign out — no location trace left behind.

### ⬇️ Load More in Tasks
The Tasks list now loads in batches instead of all at once, keeping the app responsive even in areas with tons of flags. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Load More."

---

## Improvements

### Faster, Cleaner UI
- Status history panel now respects dark mode (when we add a dark mode toggle)
- Removed visual clutter — decorative icons are hidden from screen readers so they don't interrupt navigation
- Better contrast and screen reader support on map and task screens

---

## Known Issues & Limitations

- **Coverage varies:** The map only shows where community members have reported flags. Blank areas haven't been surveyed yet — not necessarily accessible.
- **Push notifications are iOS-only:** Android support is coming in v0.3.0.
- **Invite-only beta:** We're testing with a limited group to make sure the data is solid before public launch.

## Privacy First

- We store your flag location so it shows up on the map
- We store your description and any photos you upload
- Your display name shows up on flags you report (you can change it anytime)
- **We don't sell your data.** Location is only used to place your flags on the map.
- Heatmaps (coming in a future update) won't show your individual flag — only that a region has accessibility issues
