## Method and provenance

Two read-only inventory passes catalogued the historical corpus:
- `evidence/laneJ1-historical-inventory-recent.md` — qa-reports dated ≥ 2026-07-01, the 41 qa-reports that exist ONLY in the submitted Build 33 tree, and the `design-reviews/` families (126 IDed rows).
- `evidence/laneJ2-historical-inventory-older.md` — qa-reports before 2026-07-01 plus the root-doc backlog (`APP_STORE_TODO.md`, `FEATURES.md`, `DECISIONS_LOG.md`, `TASK_GRAPH.json`, `docs/`, `security-audit/`) (164 rows).

A third pass then **verified each actionable item against current code and against the production catalog** (`evidence/laneJ3-reconciliation-draft.md` + `laneJ3-reconciliation-part2.md`, assembled here). Verification used: `git -C <worktree> show f5594171:<path>` for the submitted build, the locked `origin/main` tree for current main, and the read-only production facts in `evidence/db-proof-flags-delete-authorization.md`, `evidence/build33-backend-contract-probe.md` and `evidence/supabase-advisors.md`.

**Both J3 passes were terminated early by API rate limits**, so the tables below are what was verified before each stop. What was NOT reached is listed under Coverage gaps — it is not silently presented as clean.

A row reading `FIXED(B33) / STILL_OPEN(main)` means the repair exists only in the submitted Build 33 lineage and was never forward-ported to `main` — the single most common shape in this corpus, and a direct consequence of FDA-001.
