## Adversarial review (the pass Tier-2 couldn't complete)

10 Opus agents (one skeptic per commit, prompted to refute + a fence/completeness auditor). **Zero blockers; fence + completeness audit clean; 5 of 9 commits fully clean.** The two real findings were **fixed on-branch** in `bbf7261`:

1. **ReportFlagModal KAV placement (real):** the footer-only KAV nested inside the card measures parent-relative frames on the old architecture, breaking the keyboard-overlap math (footer under-lifted or not lifted; concrete iPhone-13 math in the review transcript). Fixed: KAV re-rooted to the backdrop wrapping the whole card, 88% cap moved onto the KAV (a % maxHeight against the auto-height KAV would silently resolve to no-cap). Both web states re-measured green.
2. **FilterPresets `rowText: {flex:1}` (real, review filed it as a nit — it was worse):** under the new column direction, flex:1 becomes a zero-flex-basis *height* — the same Bug-1 collapse shape as `renameRow`, on the branch the display path. Removed.

**Accepted trades (documented, no code change):**
- `adjustsFontSizeToFit` is not implemented by react-native-web, and `numberOfLines={1}` flips web from wrap to ellipsis (M17 pills, M3 stat/status labels, Leaderboard h1). Web at real widths is unaffected (all labels fit at 320 default; browser zoom is not fontScale); native gets the intended shrink. Same trade the sweep itself sanctioned for M18 in Tier-1.
- M16's 1.15 threshold widens the stacked-layout population beyond the literal 375 boundary — that is the sweep's own recipe ("lower type threshold ~1.15") and strictly reduces clip exposure.
- FlagDetail before/after arrow rides ~10pt above exact image center in the common one-line-caption case — stable under wrap (the old tuned offset was exactly right for one line and fully wrong on wrap); decorative glyph, rare state.
- M6 wrap left-aligns the tier pill in the extreme wrap case (wrap only engages where it previously spilled off the card).
- addIcon's redundant `fontWeight:'700'` drop: deliberate, the named Tier-1 hygiene precedent (weight comes from the SemiBold family; RN weight-on-custom-face is unreliable).
