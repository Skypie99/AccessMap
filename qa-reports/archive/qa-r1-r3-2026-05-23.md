|---|---|---|

## What's good (don't undo these)

- `parsePlaces` / `parseStreak` defenses (non-array, NaN coords, bad ISO, OOB lat/lng, unknown status strings, type mismatches) with tests for every case.
- `applyVisit` correctly handles backwards clock skew as no-op (doesn't punish DST / travel quirks).
- `computeAchievements` is pure with a defensive new-reference contract (test enforces).
- `tickVisit` skips writes when state is unchanged — preserves AsyncStorage quota.
- `updateSeqRef` request-id pattern in `refreshUpdateCount` (from PL2 of the prior QA round) correctly serializes the focus → blur → focus rapid-toggle case.
- Per-user storage-key prefixing is consistent across all 4 AsyncStorage libs.
- `MAX_PLACES`, `MAX_TRACKED`, `MAX_WATCHED` all consistently cap payload bloat.
- `accessibilityViewIsModal` on both new modal cards keeps VoiceOver inside.
- WCAG AA-verified `STATUS_COLORS` palette is reused unchanged in `statusPillCount`/`statusPillLabel`.
- The Profile streak card correctly hides when `current === 0` to avoid a "0 day streak" flash.
