Planning pass done. Here's the 5-minute version.

────────────────────────────────────
THE DIRECTION (three pillars)
────────────────────────────────────

1. TRUST — multi-user verification, status history, reporter reputation
   → so a wheelchair user can rely on what the app says
2. CONTEXT — threaded comments, multi-photo, accessibility-specific tags
   → so a single photo doesn't have to tell the whole story
3. REACH — push notifications, public web URLs, offline cache, get-directions
   → so the data gets to the people who need it, when they need it

Success in 3–4 months: a user opens a flag, reads 2–3 community comments,
sees who verified it and when, taps "Get directions" → Apple/Google Maps
opens with the route. They get a push when a watched flag flips status.
The list works offline.
