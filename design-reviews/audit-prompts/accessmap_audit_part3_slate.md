EACH PROPOSAL — header line: TITLE + WHERE (screen/component/files) · Resolves (finding IDs) · Effort S/M/L · Tier · NEEDS-SKY-DEVICE? — then the SEVEN FIELDS:
1. WHAT, vividly — the end-state, concrete enough to mockup (proposals feed Sky's mockup-gated phased execution; anything touching material follows the GLASS §9 rollout-recipe shape).

6. DEPENDENCIES / CONFLICTS — sequencing, PROTECT interactions (write them as "preserves …" statements), shared-file coordination.
7. VERIFICATION — how "done right" is checked: an arbiter re-run for ANY color/floor/ink change (name the stacks file to extend), guard tests green, blur-budget count at worst state, a11y-tree/SR re-walk, render states, or the device check.

AMBITION-TIER MIX (required): QUICK WINS (S) · MEANINGFUL UPGRADES (M/L) · and ≥3 SIGNATURE-class moves (memorable, ownable, still-in-ethos, access-first — for this product a signature that isn't accessible is a contradiction in terms). A slate of safe tweaks fails a flagship civic product.
