# LENS 2b — TOUCHABLE NAME/ROLE/STATE + ARIA-MISUSE (banked 2026-07-31)

Structural sweep of all 255 interactive elements (~1588 a11y props); auditor-judged verdicts. Baseline: 254/255 carry role + name; inputs, values, icon buttons, role validity, decorativeProps use all verified clean. Findings are the residue.

## Blocker

- **A11Y-212 (BLOCKER · 4.1.2 operability · programmatic): NotificationPreferencesScreen's 4 toggle rows announce as switches but cannot be operated by screen readers.** `NotificationPreferencesScreen.tsx:102-119`: wrapper View carries `accessibilityRole="switch"` + `a11yToggle({checked})` but **no press handler**, and the real `<Switch>` (`:114-119`) is hidden from AT (`accessibilityElementsHidden` + `no-hide-descendants`). VoiceOver/TalkBack double-tap does nothing on all four rows. This is verbatim the Alex-1 defect class (HIGH, fixed `1b53c9a`) — and the repo documents the FIX at `ProfileScreen.tsx:1611-1616` and `SettingsScreen.tsx:546-550` while this file's docblock (`:17-20`) stale-claims it mirrors SettingsScreen. Aggravator: `NotificationPreferencesScreen.test.tsx:169-173` **entrenches** the defect — it documents the missing handler and fires `valueChange` directly on the hidden Switch, so the suite can never catch it (a guard only as true as its assertion — house process law). Web is incidentally unaffected (rn-web ignores elementsHidden — F-22's flip side). Native is the submission target.

## High

(b) AddressSearchModal `:305-329` (`role="text"` container holding the Try-again button — role compounds it). (c) FlagDetailModal `:1078-1095` (label swallows "Add an after photo").
