## F6 — Signature deepening

**Lens verdict.** The signature is real and it is already the brand: blinded readers, given nothing but pixels, independently named it — R5 ("cover the logo and yes, I can identify it… a civic reporting tool whose brand is its severity scale"), R1 ("somebody decided how this app talks about danger and then never wavered"), R7 ("number + word + color, everywhere, consistently"). The grammar's failures at HEAD are not decoration creep — I found zero ramp-as-mood violations — they are *decay at the edges*: the deepest trust surface (FlagDetailModal) speaks only "Severity 4," the signed-in mirror surfaces (Profile pill, My Reports, My Watched) drop the word or the digit entirely, and the first screen's Recent dots are the one disc that never learned to carry its number (R3: "put the numbers in those dots too"). Candidates I examined and killed: a severity digit on the map pin (the 30px teardrop already carries four channels — fill=severity, glyph=category, check=resolved, double-ring=anon — a fifth evicts legibility; RESTRAINT: FAIL, killed); pre-tinting the report picker's five resting buttons with the ramp (adds five saturated discs to the sheet R7 already calls the noisiest moment, and dissolves the selected-state pop R3 praised; RESTRAINT: FAIL on visual-entropy grounds, killed); severity/category tones on outcome toasts (ramp-as-mood by definition, killed); grammar-speaking zero states (already answered — the PROTECT-2 card's "Any severity" chip is the honest, restrained form; no change). What survives below is a short ladder: finish dressing the surfaces the grammar already owns.

### PROTECT nominations

- **The ramp's mode-independence.** The discs are byte-identical in light and dark (`nearby-modal` light/dark pair [web-approximated]; PROTECT-16 literals [code-inferred]). The signature never re-costumes per theme — freeze it.
- **The ink fork as ONE system (P2 ledger question — answered).** Dark ink 1–4 / white 5 reads as deliberate at HEAD: the ink follows the ramp's darkening, and the sole white digit makes 5 pop as the extreme (legend + nearby captures, both themes). Never "fix" it to uniform ink; it is a designed asymmetry with measured AA behind every pair (theme.ts:541–545).
- **The wording spine.** `severity` (theme.ts:533) → `SEVERITY_LABELS`/`severityColor` (flags.ts derives, :1208–1223) → `severityA11y` (a11yText.ts:17) → `heatmapSeverity` labels (theme.ts:575). One vocabulary, four consumers, zero drift found at HEAD. Extensions only.
- **The pin's four-channel encoding is at capacity.** Fill=severity, glyph=category, check=resolved, double-ring=anonymous, in 30px with the GLASS §12.4 hairline union (PlatformMap.tsx:278–298, PlatformMap.web.tsx:138–160). Never add a fifth channel (including a digit) — the number's homes are the callout, the list, and the legend.
- **Outcome beats stay severity-free.** FlashBanner (`success`/`info`) and LiveStatusRegion are neutral by design — the ramp never becomes toast mood. This is the hard rule expressed as architecture; freeze it.
- **Reward gold never wears the disc.** Civic Gold (`#FBB024`), tier metals, and trophies share the amber *band* with the ramp's low end but never borrow the grammar's exclusive *form* — no reward element renders as a numbered/colored disc (TierIcon=Medal/Gem, Achievements=Lucide glyphs, Leaderboard=Trophy [code-inferred]). Write the rule down so it survives future gamification work.
- **The empty-filter card's "Any severity" chip** (MapScreen.tsx:1042–1043) — the zero state already speaks the grammar in its most restrained honest form; the brief's "no severity-5 barriers here" fantasy is correctly NOT built. Keep it exactly this quiet.
