## F2 — Rhythm & optical alignment

**Lens verdict.** The vertical rhythm inside surfaces is this app's quiet strength — the Legend's five-row severity cadence, the Nearby card grammar, and the editorial eyebrow/display/subtitle trio are set with real care and survive dark mode byte-for-byte (`web-approximated`). The failures are almost all *between* layers, not within them: nothing in the overlay stratum (callout, live-status pill, FAB, bulk bar) knows where anything else is, so the app's most trust-sensitive text gets composited under chrome — R5's "nothing reserves space for anything" is the accurate diagnosis. The S8 "one header rhythm" promise is real but only ~80% kept: five screens wear the header at four different insets, the Map strands its menu circle mid-air, and the guest Profile drops the family entirely. Two honesty rails for the skeptic pass: (1) the serif face in the light Home captures (and R7's dark-panel serif) is a cold-font capture artifact suspect per orientation §4.12 — `App.tsx:211` returns null until `useFonts` settles (`code-inferred`), so no baseline-rhythm judgment below leans on those panes; (2) the zoom130/200 stress captures were made by an emulation that shrinks CSS layout *without* updating the `Dimensions` API, so `Dimensions`-keyed defenses (M16's compact stack) are bypassed in captures while layout-keyed ones (M18 auto-fit, S16 fade measurement) genuinely ran — findings below are graded with that split stated per claim.
