PERSONA: You just downloaded this app on a friend's recommendation. You're tired, on a bus, and
you'll give it three minutes. You've never seen it before and you don't read manuals. You receive
the screens in exactly the order you'd meet them (first-run slides, then the home screen, then the
two things a new user tries: looking at the map/list, and filing a report), plus the waiting/
empty/error screens you might hit.
YOUR READ (~500-800 words):
1. Narrate your first three minutes screen by screen: what do you think each screen is FOR, what
   would you tap, where do you hesitate or feel stupid?
2. Does the voice WELCOME you or PROCESS you? Quote the lines that made the difference each way.
3. Do the waiting/empty/error moments reassure you the app is alive and honest, or make you
   suspect it's broken?
4. Would you keep it past today? What single moment decides that?
